# LF Academy Design Philosophy: 'Joyful Precision'

## The Movement
Joyful Precision is a visual language born at the intersection of Hong Kong's rigorous primary mathematics education and the playful wonder of childhood discovery. It treats every formula, diagram, and practice question as an invitation to play. The design speaks to 8-12 year olds—warm, rounded, tactile—while maintaining structural clarity for SSPA standards.

## Space and Form
Generous breathing room surrounds every element. Questions float in soft, rounded cards (12-16px radius). White space is the silence between notes, giving young minds processing room. Grid is invisible but guiding: objective → concept → practice → challenge. Claymorphism shadows create subtle 3D depth.

## Color and Material
Hong Kong-inspired palette: warm coral (#FF6B6B), South China Sea teal (#4ECDC4), market yellow (#FFD93D), egg tart cream (#FFF8F0). Progress green (#6BCB77). Every color carries meaning: coral=important, teal=examples, yellow=tips, green=complete.

## Typography
Noto Sans TC for Chinese—modern clarity for complex notation. Baloo 2 for numerals—rounded, playful. Clear hierarchy: headings 1.5-2x body. Mathematical expressions given visual weight.

## Micro-Interactions
Gentle hover scales (1.02x), soft pulse on correct, encouraging nudge on mistakes. Progress bars create momentum. Touch targets >=44px. WCAG AA contrast compliance.

## Craft
Every pixel placed with calligraphic care. Design serves learning—this is educational architecture, not decoration.
